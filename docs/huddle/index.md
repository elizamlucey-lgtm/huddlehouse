# Welcome to Huddle

Good work happens together!

Huddle is the team communication space in Huddlehouse. It's where teammates share updates, ask questions, exchange ideas, and work through problems.

Whether you're coordinating a release, investigating customer feedback, or asking an engineering question, Huddle helps keep important conversations connected to the work.

## Meet your Huddle guides

### Nova — Your team organizer

Nova helps the team stay coordinated. Nova shares reminders, connects conversations to projects, and makes sure important decisions don't get lost.

### Pip — Your quality explorer

Pip is curious about everything! Pip asks questions, spots potential gaps, and reminds the team to check details before sharing information.

## How conversations become improvements

A useful conversation should lead somewhere.

1. A teammate identifies a question, problem, or opportunity.
2. The team discusses the issue and gathers relevant information.
3. The right people verify the facts.
4. A follow-up task is created in Projects when work is needed.
5. The team shares the outcome when the work is complete.

> Nova's reminder: A conversation is valuable when it helps someone take the next step.

## Good Huddle habits

- Use the channel that best matches the topic.
- Explain the problem clearly.
- Share links and supporting information.
- Keep technical claims grounded in verified information.
- Summarize important decisions.
- Create a project task when a conversation requires follow-up.
- Share completed improvements with the people who need to know.

Pip's favorite rule: If a conversation contains an important decision, make sure the decision is easy to find later!
