# Getting Started with Huddlehouse

Welcome to Huddlehouse! This guide introduces the platform and walks you through the basics of setting up your workspace.

By the end of this guide, you'll understand how to navigate Huddlehouse, access its primary features, and find the resources you need to get started.

## Before you begin

To get started, you'll need:

- Access to an existing Huddlehouse workspace
- A valid user account provided by your workspace administrator
- A supported web browser
- An internet connection

If you haven't received an invitation to your workspace, contact your organization's Huddlehouse administrator.

## 1. Sign in to your workspace

1. Open the Huddlehouse sign-in page provided by your organization.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password or complete your organization's configured sign-in process.
4. Select **Sign in**.

After authentication, Huddlehouse opens your workspace.

> **Note:** Your sign-in experience may vary depending on the authentication settings configured by your organization.

## 2. Navigate your workspace

The Huddlehouse workspace provides access to five primary product areas.

| Product area | Description |
|---|---|
| Huddle | Communicate and collaborate with teammates |
| Projects | Organize tasks, milestones, and project workflows |
| Library | Access shared documentation and knowledge resources |
| Developer Hub | Explore API references, integrations, and developer resources |
| Huddlehouse Academy | Access learning materials, tutorials, and training |

Select a product area from the main navigation to explore its available resources.

## 3. Explore the Library

The Library provides a central location for your organization's documentation and shared knowledge.

Use the Library to:

- Find product guides and instructional content
- Access internal team documentation
- Review shared procedures and resources
- Learn how to complete common tasks

Your access to individual resources may depend on your organization's workspace permissions.

## 4. Explore Projects

The Projects area helps teams organize and track their work.

Depending on your workspace configuration, you may be able to:

- View active projects
- Review assigned tasks
- Track project milestones
- Follow task progress
- Collaborate with project members

If you cannot access a project, contact its owner or your workspace administrator.

## 5. Find learning resources

Visit Huddlehouse Academy to explore available courses, tutorials, and guided learning experiences.

Academy content helps users understand product features and develop the skills needed to work effectively within Huddlehouse.

## 6. Get help

If you need assistance:

1. Search the Library for a relevant guide or troubleshooting article.
2. Review available resources in Huddlehouse Academy.
3. Contact your workspace administrator if you encounter an account or permission issue.

## What's next?

Now that you're familiar with the Huddlehouse workspace, explore the documentation for the product areas most relevant to your role.

- [Browse the Library](index.md) for product guidance.
- Explore Projects to understand team workflows.
- Visit Developer Hub for technical resources.
- Start a course in Huddlehouse Academy.
- [Review the Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md) if you encounter an issue.
