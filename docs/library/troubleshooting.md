# Huddlehouse Troubleshooting Guide

This guide covers common issues users may encounter when accessing or navigating Huddlehouse.

Use the troubleshooting steps below to identify potential causes and resolve common problems.

## Sign-in issues

### I can't sign in to my workspace

**Possible causes:**
- Incorrect email address or password
- Expired or invalid authentication session
- Account access has been disabled
- Organization-specific authentication requirements

**Resolution:**

1. Verify that you're using the email address associated with your Huddlehouse account.
2. Check that your password is entered correctly.
3. If your organization uses single sign-on (SSO), follow its configured sign-in process.
4. Close and reopen your browser, then try signing in again.
5. Contact your workspace administrator if the issue continues.

## Workspace access issues

### I can't access a product area

**Possible causes:**
- Your account does not have the required permissions.
- The resource is restricted to specific workspace members.
- Your administrator has changed your access.

**Resolution:**

1. Verify that you're signed in to the correct workspace.
2. Refresh the page and try accessing the product area again.
3. Contact your workspace administrator to verify your permissions.

> **Note:** Access to Huddle, Projects, Library, Developer Hub, and Academy may vary based on your organization's configuration.

## Project issues

### I can't find a project

**Possible causes:**
- The project is not shared with your account.
- You're viewing the wrong workspace.
- The project has been archived or removed.

**Resolution:**

1. Verify that you're in the correct workspace.
2. Check whether the project is available in your project list.
3. Contact the project owner to confirm that you have access.

## Documentation issues

### I can't find a document in the Library

**Possible causes:**
- The document has been moved or renamed.
- Your account does not have permission to view it.
- Your search terms do not match the document's title or content.

**Resolution:**

1. Try searching with a different keyword.
2. Browse the relevant Library category.
3. Verify that you're signed in to the correct workspace.
4. Contact the document owner if you still cannot locate the resource.

## Browser and display issues

### Huddlehouse is not displaying correctly

**Possible causes:**
- An outdated browser version
- Cached browser data
- A temporary browser or network issue

**Resolution:**

1. Refresh the page.
2. Verify that your browser is up to date.
3. Clear your browser cache if the issue persists.
4. Open Huddlehouse in a supported browser.
5. Check your internet connection.

## When to contact support

If the troubleshooting steps do not resolve your issue, contact your organization's Huddlehouse administrator or designated support team.

When reporting a problem, include:

- A description of the issue
- The product area where the issue occurred
- The steps you took before the issue appeared
- Any error message displayed
- Your browser and operating system

Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive account information in support requests.
